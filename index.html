How it is worked outformulas

The shaft drives the stair

W = (shaft width − well) / 2
n = ceil(H / R_max)      R = H / n
n₁ = ceil(n/2)           n₂ = n − n₁
going = shaft length − door landing − half landing
T = going / (max(n₁,n₂) − 1)

The tread is what the shaft length allows, capped at the preferred tread; anything left over deepens the landings rather than stretching the steps.

Three headroom checks

under the flight   H − waist·√(T²+R²)/T
over the half landing   H − half slab
over the door landing   H − floor slab

The two flights sit on opposite sides of the well, so a flight never passes under the other flight of the same level — it passes under the same flight of the level above.

What the code fixes

  • Width ≥ 1200 mm to 2000 persons, ≥ 1420 mm above; handrails may project 100 mm each side.
  • Riser 100–180 mm, tread ≥ 280 mm, uniform within 10 mm in a flight.
  • Headroom ≥ 2030 mm; no more than 3660 mm of height between landings.
  • Landing width ≥ stair width, and need not exceed 1220 mm along a straight run.
